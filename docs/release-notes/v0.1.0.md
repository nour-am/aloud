The first public release of **Aloud**, an audiobook player for jailbroken Kindles.

![Aloud on a Kindle Paperwhite](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NoureldinAmer/aloud/main/docs/media/hero.png)

[▶ Watch the 36-second demo with sound](https://github.com/NoureldinAmer/aloud/blob/main/docs/media/demo.mp4)

### Highlights

- Plays your own DRM-free audiobooks: single files with chapters (`.m4b`, `.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.ogg`, `.opus`, `.flac`) and folders of chapter files.
- **Play in background** keeps listening while you read in the Kindle's reader or KOReader. Control it from KUAL or the included KOReader plugin.
- Never loses your place: atomic saves with a backup copy, 2-second rewind on resume.
- Speed from 0.75× to 3× without changing pitch. Sleep timer with fade-out, including *end of chapter*.
- Titles and chapters in any of Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, Arabic (right-to-left), Chinese and Japanese.
- Light on the hardware: 4.7 MB peak memory on an 8.6-hour M4B, no CPU use while paused.

### Install

You need a jailbroken Kindle on firmware 5.x with the hotfix ([kindlemodding.org](https://kindlemodding.org)).

1. Download `aloud-0.1.0.zip` below.
2. Unzip it onto the root of the Kindle's USB drive.
3. Copy audiobooks into the new `audiobooks/` folder.
4. Eject and tap **Aloud** in your library, or open it from KUAL.

Check the download against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

### Devices

Tested on a Paperwhite 5 (FW 5.19.2). Every other Bluetooth Kindle on FW 5.x, and the Kindle Touch's jack and speaker, should work but haven't been confirmed yet. A [device report](https://github.com/NoureldinAmer/aloud/issues/new?template=device_report.yml) helps a lot, whether it works or not.

### Known limitations

- No Audible or other DRM-protected books.
- No Korean font yet; very rare Chinese/Japanese characters show as "?".
- The interface is in English.
